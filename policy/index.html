Service, Return, & Warranty Policy

Geno’s Euro Garage LLC | Mobile Service Terms & Conditions

1. Diagnostic & Travel Fees

  • Base Call Out Fee: The initial base / diagnostic fee covers site travel and vehicle assessment. This fee is non-refundable once the mechanic arrives at the location.
  • Extended Service Area Travel Surcharge: Services requested outside of our standard service radius are subject to an additional travel fee. This surcharge will be disclosed and agreed upon prior to dispatch.
  • Unserviceable Locations: Service requires a flat, hard surface (concrete or asphalt) and a safe working environment. If service must be canceled due to unsafe site conditions (e.g., steep slope, unsafe roadside, aggressive animals), the standard call-out fee still applies.

2. Parts & Returns

  • Restocking Fees: Unopened, standard parts returned due to customer cancellation within 14 days of purchase are subject to a 20% supplier restocking fee.
  • Parts Cost Protection: In the event a purchased part does not resolve your vehicle's issue after proper diagnosis, you will only be responsible for the labor completed. Geno’s Euro Garage LLC will absorb the full cost of the part and issue a 100% refund for it with zero restocking fees.
  • Refund Processing Time: All approved refunds will be processed back to the original method of payment within 3 to 5 business days. Please note that once Geno’s Euro Garage LLC initiates the refund transfer, the exact posting date depends on your financial institution and is beyond our direct control.
  • Final Sale Items: Electrical components, special-order European parts, fluids, and parts that have already been installed or plugged in are strictly non-refundable, except as explicitly outlined under shop-approved unresolved diagnostic repairs above.
  • Customer-Supplied Parts: We do not offer warranty, guarantee fitment, or accept returns on parts supplied directly by the client. Labor required to replace defective customer-supplied parts will be billed at standard rates.
